Kirtan Guide

Framework of The Daily Programme

at Śrī Chaitanya Sāraswat Maṭh, Nabadwīp

The first line of each kirtan is given along with the its page number.

At the beginning of each ārati, the Pañcha Tattva mantra is sung:

Śrī-Kṛṣṇa-Chaitanya, Prabhu Nityānanda,
Śrī-Advaita, Gadādhara, Śrīvāsādi Gaura-bhakta-vṛnda

Each ārati and parikramā ends with the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahāmantra:

Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare
Hare Rāma Hare Rāma, Rāma Rāma Hare Hare

The Daily Morning Programme

Begins with Śrīla Gurudev’s ārati at his Samādhi Mandir – The Temple of Love and Affection:

jaya jaya gurudever ārati ujjvala 44

Then Śrīla Guru Mahārāj’s ārati at his Samādhi Mandir – The Temple of Union in Separation:

jaya ‘guru-mahārāja’ yati-rājeśvara 46

Followed by the ārati of Śrī Śrī Guru-Gaurāṅga-Gāndharvā-Govindasundar-jīu (the Deities of Śrī Chaitanya Sāraswat Maṭh):

kali-kukkura-kadana yadi chāo he 49

yaśomatī-nandana vraja-vara-nāgara 50

jaya śachī-nandana sura-muni-vandana 51

Then parikramā of the three Temples:

gurudeva! kṛpā-bindu diyā 52

udila aruṇa pūrava-bhāge 53

jīva jāga, jīva jāga 55

Followed by offering obeisances to Śrīmatī Tulasī Devī:

vṛndāyai tulasī-devyai 26

Immediately followed by obeisances to the Vaiṣṇavas:

vāñchhā-kalpa-tarubhyaś cha 22

and sometimes:

sakala vaiṣṇava-pade mora namaskāra
ithe kichhu aparādha nahuka āmāra
haiyachen haiben prabhura jata bhakta jana
vandanā kari’ āmi sabāra charaṇa

“I respectfully bow to the lotus feet of all the Vaiṣṇavas, praying that there is no offence in my attempt to please them. To all Vaiṣṇavas who have been, and all Vaiṣṇavas who shall be, I offer my obeisances to their lotus feet.”

Finally devotees offer obeisances to Śrīla Gurudev and Śrīla Guru Mahārāj at their Samādhī Mandirs, and to the Deities. Then beside the Temple, facing east they offer obeisances to Śrīmatī Gaṅgadevi:

sadya pātaka saṁhantri sadya duḥkha vināśinī
sukhadā bhaktidā gaṅgā gaṅgeva paramāgati

“O Gaṅgā, destroyer of sins in an instant, destroyer of sorrow in an instant, giver of happiness, giver of devotion – O Gaṅgā, you are certainly the supreme refuge.”

THE MORNING KĪRTANS begin with:

Recitation of the Vandana prayers 15

Followed by the kīrtans:

saṁsāra-dāvānala-līḍha-loka 56

śrī-kṛṣṇa-chaitanya prabhu jīve dayā kari 59

bhaja re bhaja re āmār mana ati manda 60

After which is a reading from Śrī Chaitanya Bhāgavata in Bengali.

Closing with:

hari haraye namaḥ kṛṣṇa yādavāya namaḥ 65

After morning Prasādam is a class in English (or Spanish, etc.) established by Śrīla Gurudev and held on Śrīla Guru Mahārāj’s Samādhī Mandir.

THE MIDDAY PROGRAMME

commences with offering bhoga, while singing:

bhaja bhakata-vatsala śrī-gaurahari 66

Followed by Śrīla Gurudev’s ārati and Śrīla Guru Mahārāj’s ārati at their respective Samadhi Mandirs (as in the morning).

Then ārati of the Deities:

yaśomatī-nandana vraja-vara-nāgara 50

jaya śachī-nandana sura-muni-vandana 51

Before honouring Prasadam, the devotees chant:

mahāprasāde govinde 69

śarīra avidyā-jāla 69

(but on days when no grains are taken, only the first prayer is chanted.)

MID-AFTERNOON: reading from Śrīmad Bhāgavatam in Bengali.

THE EVENING PROGRAMME

begins with Śrīla Gurudev’s and Śrīla Guru Mahārāj’s āratis at their respective Samādhi Mandirs.

Then ārati of the Deities:

(kibā) jaya jaya gorāchā̐der āratika śobhā 73

jayare jayare jaya gaura-sarasvatī 75

Followed by parikramā of all three Temples:

gurudeva! kṛpā-bindu diyā 52

kabe śrī-chaitanya more karibena dayā 77

namo namaḥ tulasī mahārāṇī 78

Then, as in the morning programme, devotees offer obeisances to Śrīmatī Tulasī Devī, the Vaiṣṇavas, the three Temples, and Gaṅga Devī.

EVENING KĪRTANS:

Recitation of the Vandana prayers 15

Followed by the songs:

śrī-guru-charaṇa-padma 79

śrī-kṛṣṇa-chaitanya prabhu dayā kara more 80

(ohe) vaiṣṇava ṭhākura dayāra sāgara 81

Followed by a selection, most of which are included in this Kīrtan Guide.

Daily, usually near the end, is sung:

sujanārbuda-rādhita-pāda-yugaṁ 83

Which is followed daily by 10 verses from Śrī Śrī Prema-dhāma-deva Stotram beginning with verse 1 each Saturday.

deva-siddha-mukta-yukta 86

Followed by a Bengali reading from Śrī Chaitanya Charitāmṛta.

Concluding with:

hari haraye namaḥ kṛṣṇa yādavāya namaḥ 65

AdditIonal kĪrtans chanted on special occasions:

During the month of Kārtik

additional kirtans are chanted.

The following are added to the morning programme:

nadīyā-nagare nitāi neche neche gāya re 235

nagare nagare gorā gāya 239

namāmīśvaraṁ sachchidānanda-rūpaṁ 272

rādhe jaya jaya mādhava-dayite 287

And added to the evening programme is Mahāprabhu’s Śrī Śikṣāṣṭakam, with Śrīla Bhaktivinod Ṭhākur’s accompanying compositions:

cheto-darpaṇa-mārjanaṁ pages 192 - 207

pīta-varaṇa kali-pāvana gorā 192

On the Appearance Day of a guru in our lineage

the following is also chanted during the morning and/or evening seated programme. This song is often begun from verse five or six:

kṛṣṇa haite chatur-mukha 221

On the Appearance Day of Śrī Gaurāṅga Sundar

the following are sung leading up to moonrise:

aruṇa vasane sonāra sūraja 141

chaudda-śata sāta-śake māsa ye phālguna 247

On the Appearance Days of Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s Līlā-avatārs

the Śrī Daśāvatār Stotram is sung:

pralaya-payodhi-jale 231

On the Disappearance Days of Gurus

from our lineage or other worshipable Vaiṣṇavas:

ye ānila prema-dhana 119

also the following may be sung:

ei-bāra karuṇā kara vaiṣṇava gosāñi 120

On Harivāsar Tithi

whether it falls on an Ekādaśī or a Mahādvādaśī.

In the morning programme:

śrī-hari-vāsare hari-kīrtana-vidhāna 243

and in the evening:

śuddha-bhakata-charaṇa-reṇu 245

Ākhars (lit. ornaments) may be sung during or after songs. Throughout this book, ākhars are in non-bold text enclosed within brackets.
